# Code of Conduct

GitHub Achievement Hunt is committed to a welcoming, respectful, and constructive community.

## Our standards

Help make participation positive by:

- Being considerate, respectful, and open to differing viewpoints.
- Giving constructive feedback and accepting it graciously.
- Focusing on what is best for learners and the project.
- Respecting contributors' privacy and crediting work accurately.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, intimidation, publishing private information without permission, and deliberately disruptive or misleading contributions.

## Raising a concern

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact the repository maintainers privately through GitHub. Please do not post personal or sensitive details in a public issue. Maintainers will review reports and may take appropriate action, including removing content or restricting participation.

This code of conduct applies to project spaces and to public interactions when someone is representing the project.
