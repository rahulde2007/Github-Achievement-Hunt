# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

The GitHub Achievement Hunt community aims to be welcoming, respectful, and useful to everyone, regardless of experience or background. We are committed to keeping project spaces constructive and focused on learning, accurate information, and meaningful contributions.

## Where this applies

This Code of Conduct applies to project spaces—including issues and pull requests—and to public interactions when someone is representing the project or its community.

## Expected behavior

Community members are expected to:

- Treat others with courtesy, empathy, and respect.
- Discuss ideas and work constructively; critique the contribution, not the person.
- Welcome questions, different perspectives, and good-faith corrections.
- Give accurate attribution and respect other people's privacy.
- Follow project guidance and GitHub's rules when participating.
- Help keep achievement information honest: distinguish verified requirements from uncertain or commonly associated activity.

## Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable conduct includes:

- Harassment, discrimination, insults, threats, intimidation, or personal attacks.
- Unwelcome sexual attention or deliberately humiliating behavior.
- Publishing or threatening to publish another person's private information without permission.
- Repeated disruption, trolling, or knowingly misleading participation.
- Misrepresenting authorship, fabricating contributions, or encouraging unsafe actions to pursue an achievement.
- Retaliating against someone for raising a concern in good faith.

## Reporting a concern

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code, contact a repository maintainer privately through GitHub. You can identify the project owner from the [repository](https://github.com/rahulde2007/Github-Achievement-Hunt). Do not include private or sensitive details in a public issue.

When practical, include links or context that will help the maintainers understand what happened, when it occurred, and which project space was involved. Share only information that is necessary. If there is an immediate risk to someone's safety, contact the appropriate local emergency or support service.

## Enforcement

Maintainers will review reports and determine an appropriate response based on the circumstances, impact, and pattern of behavior. They will make a reasonable effort to hear relevant context and handle reports with discretion; absolute confidentiality cannot be guaranteed.

Possible responses include a private reminder or warning, a request to edit or remove content, temporary restrictions on participation, or permanent exclusion from project spaces. A serious violation may result in immediate action. Maintainers may remove content that violates this Code or GitHub's rules.

Where appropriate, maintainers will explain the decision to the people directly involved. A person who believes an enforcement decision was made in error may contact the maintainers privately with a concise appeal and any relevant context.

## Scope and interpretation

This Code complements, and does not replace, GitHub's terms and policies. Maintainers are responsible for applying it fairly and consistently within project spaces.
